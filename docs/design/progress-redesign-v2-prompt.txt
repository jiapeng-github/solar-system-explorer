Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity redesigned “进度” page for Chinese astronomy tablet app “太阳系漫游”, a single landscape 16:10 app screen, about 1600x1000.
Reference image: approved home-concept-v2, use only its premium deep-navy space aesthetic, white Chinese typography, cyan actions, thin blue borders and realistic planet imagery. This is a NEW learning-progress screen, not a homepage hero.
Primary goal: immediately show what has been explored, which experiments are done, and the next useful action. Elegant educational journey, not an enterprise analytics dashboard. No giant decorative planet taking away content space. Flat app screenshot, no device frame or OS title bar.
Consistent illustrative state MUST hold everywhere: 3 of 8 planets visited (水星、金星、地球 only); 1 of 3 experiments completed (自转与昼夜 only); 0 of 2 learning tasks completed. Mars is next. Do not imply unvisited planets or unfinished experiments are locked.
Layout, all visible in one spacious tablet screen:
Top slim navigation like reference: orbit logo and “太阳系漫游”, five tabs “出发  探索  实验室  比较  进度”; 进度 cyan active underline. No settings.
Below a modest title row “你的学习旅程” with subtitle “每一次探索，都让宇宙更清晰一点。”
Top summary band ~108px tall: three compact outlined metric tiles on left, one wider next-step card on right. Metrics “3 / 8” caption “已探索行星”, “1 / 3” caption “已完成实验”, “0 / 2” caption “已完成任务”. Cyan numerals with subdued denominators, restrained small icons. Next-step card features a small detailed Mars on left, eyebrow “推荐下一步”, title “继续认识岩石行星”, caption “下一站：火星”, cyan rounded button “探索火星 →” on right. Slightly stronger cyan edge; single dominant CTA for the whole page.
Middle section 1, full-width outlined panel with section header “行星足迹” and right helper “点击行星，继续探索”. Eight evenly spaced recognizable planet miniatures in one row, each with clear Chinese name beneath: 水星 金星 地球 火星 木星 土星 天王星 海王星. Mercury, Venus, Earth have subtle cyan circular halo and a small check badge, status “已探索”. Other five have no check and status “待探索”; keep their colors recognizable rather than grayed to invisibility. Mars may have a thin dashed cyan outline and small “下一站” label instead of 待探索. Ringed Saturn fits its cell. Planet graphics decorative scale, not a true-size chart. No orbit line connecting planets in a misleading physical diagram.
Section 2 title “互动实验” and right small “已完成 1 / 3”. Three equal horizontal cards, each with a small educational planet illustration on left and concise title/status on right. Card A title “自转与昼夜”, a half-lit Earth icon, cyan check pill “已完成”, small secondary action “再看一次 →”. Card B title “公转与一年”, Earth orbiting a small Sun icon, muted status “待完成”, small outlined action “开始实验 →”. Card C title “四季与地轴”, Earth with tilted axis icon, muted status “待完成”, small outlined action “开始实验 →”. No checkmarks on B/C. Do not add invented scores or time estimates.
Section 3 title “学习任务” with small contextual text “探索 → 比较 → 验证”. Two equal wide compact cards side by side.
Left task card: eyebrow “任务 01”, title “认识岩石行星”; text “已探索 3 / 4 · 指定比较待完成”; three-step mini path “探索行星 — 比较自转 — 回答问题”, only exploration step partially cyan; short outlined action “继续任务 →”. Small tasteful thumbnails of Mercury/Venus/Earth/Mars, no task-complete badge.
Right task card: eyebrow “任务 02”, title “寻找巨行星”; text “已探索 0 / 4 · 指定比较待完成”; three-step mini path “探索行星 — 比较大小 — 回答问题”, all subdued; outlined action “开始任务 →”. Small tasteful Jupiter/Saturn/Uranus/Neptune thumbnails. No task-complete badge.
Footer: left small save/check icon and “学习记录仅保存在本机”; right quiet low-prominence text button “重置学习记录”. Reset must not resemble a primary CTA and should not distract. No cloud sync, sign-in, streaks, rankings, trophies, points, percentage aggregation or invented achievements.
Visual priorities: comfortable touch targets, aligned grids, balanced whitespace, crisp readable simplified Chinese, clean rounded rectangles, restrained cyan outlines, realistic small globe illustrations. Dark navy background with sparse stars and faint blue nebula at outer edges, nearly solid dark text panels for readability. Gold accents only for Sun icons. Large title but smaller than homepage hero. Ensure whole screen fits within 16:10 with ~30px margins, no clipping or cramped text. The recommended next step, planet status, experiments and tasks must be mutually consistent. Render the final UI concept only.
