# Visual asset credits — v0.3.0

Downloaded 2026-10-08. All runtime assets are bundled; the app performs no network requests.

## NASA imagery

- `assets/textures/earth.png`: [Blue Marble, NASA SVS 2915](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915). NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio; Blue Marble data courtesy Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory. [Original 2048 × 1024 PNG](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a002900/a002915/bluemarble-2048.png).
- `assets/textures/moon.jpg`: [CGI Moon Kit, NASA SVS 4720](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720), Ernie Wright; NASA/GSFC/ASU, LRO/LROC teams. [Original 2048 × 1024 JPEG](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg).
- `assets/textures/clouds.jpg`: [Blue Marble: Clouds](https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57747/blue-marble-clouds/77558l), NASA GSFC, Reto Stöckli; enhancements by Robert Simmon. [Original global cloud composite](https://eoimages.gsfc.nasa.gov/images/imagerecords/57000/57747/cloud_combined_2048.jpg). Historic composite, not current weather.

Files retained as downloaded. Runtime changes: spherical mapping, lighting, ocean-color enhancement, cloud alpha from brightness, atmosphere rim, mipmapping and thumbnail rendering. [NASA imagery usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). No NASA logo or endorsement is used.

## Solar System Scope / INOVE

Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune textures by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, [Solar Textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Original URLs: `https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_<name>.jpg`, where `<name>` is `mercury`, `venus_atmosphere`, `mars`, `jupiter`, `saturn`, `uranus`, `neptune`.

Source JPEGs retained as downloaded. Adaptations: spherical mapping, dynamic lighting, mipmapping, rendered thumbnail PNGs in `assets/thumbnails/`. The maps are artistic adaptations of planetary data; the publisher notes enhanced colors and inferred terrain where coverage is incomplete. They are not measurement maps or guaranteed natural-color photographs. Credit, license URL and adaptation notice are also available offline in the app's “资料与说明”.

## Project-created

Planet meshes, procedural Saturn rings, atmosphere and cloud shaders, star field, orbit logo and UI are project-generated. Saturn rings are illustrative geometry, without particulate physics or accurate ring shadows. `docs/design/approved-exploration.png` is the user-approved AI-generated concept; it is a reference only, excluded from the application. The app is rendered from live 3D geometry and interactive controls.

## UI audio — v0.19.0

`assets/audio/01_tap.wav` through `13_reset.wav` are original procedural synthesis generated by `tools/design_ui_audio.py` for this project, approved by the user. No third-party audio samples are used. Runtime files are PCM16, mono, 48 kHz; the design and audition reel remain under `docs/design/audio-v1` and are excluded from exports.

## Background music — v0.20.0

`assets/music/starlight-voyage-loop.wav` (《星海慢航》 / Starlight Voyage) is original procedural synthesis generated by `tools/design_background_music.py`, approved by the user. No third-party samples or recordings are used. The app loops the lossless 60-second stereo PCM master; the faded audition is excluded from exports.
